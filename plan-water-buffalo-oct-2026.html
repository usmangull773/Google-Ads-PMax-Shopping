01

What we found

A first pass over the storefront and product data, before any account access. The products and reviews are strong. The product data Google reads is holding them back.

Product identifiers are Amazon ASINs, not GTINs

High impact
waterbuffalo.com/products/road-runner-hydration-pack

The Shopify barcode field feeds the GTIN attribute in Merchant Center. On the Road Runner, every variant holds an Amazon ASIN. Google cannot match these to a catalog product, which limits how often Shopping and Performance Max show the listing and can trigger identifier warnings.

VariantBarcode field nowWhat Merchant Center expects
BlackB0777J7SQY12 or 13 digit UPC / EAN
RedB07FTQXTP912 or 13 digit UPC / EAN
Navy BlueB07FTSB74812 or 13 digit UPC / EAN
Light BlueB0777N2TLP12 or 13 digit UPC / EAN

Titles and URLs are written for Amazon, not for Google Shopping

High impact
waterbuffalo.com product pages

Product titles are in full capitals, which Google flags as excessive capitalization. Two product URLs are full Amazon titles. Shopping titles need the brand, product type, size and key spec in the first 70 characters, because that is all a shopper sees.

Now

  • Title: WATER BUFFALO ROAD RUNNER HYDRATION PACK
  • URL: /water-buffalo-hydration-running-vest-quiver-hydration-vest-running-hydration-vest-with-dual-water-bottles-lightweight-water-backpack-for-running-trail-running-marathons

After the fix

  • Feed title: Water Buffalo Road Runner Hydration Pack 2L, Lightweight Running Backpack, Red
  • URL: /quiver-hydration-running-vest, with a 301 redirect from the old one

Sold out colours and reviews need to be wired into the campaigns

Medium impact
Shopify inventory, Merchant Center and Performance Max

Black and Light Blue are sold out on the Road Runner. Feed availability should update automatically and spend should move to in-stock colors. The 1,275 reviews should flow to Google as product ratings, so the stars show on Shopping ads.

1

Audit the account and check purchase tracking

Confirm GA4 and Google Ads purchase events fire once per order with the correct value, check enhanced conversions, and review the last 90 days of search terms, product performance and wasted spend.

Why: ROAS decisions are only as good as the revenue data behind them.

2

Fix identifiers and titles in the feed

Replace ASINs with real UPCs, rewrite titles in title case with brand, type, capacity and colour first, and fill in product type, Google category, colour and size attributes.

Why: matched GTINs and clear titles widen the searches each product can show for.

3

Connect reviews and keep stock in sync

Send the existing reviews to Google as product ratings, sync availability daily, and use a supplemental feed to tag sold out and low margin items.

Why: stars lift click through rate, and no budget goes to products shoppers cannot buy.

4

Rebuild Performance Max and Shopping by product role

Hero products (Road Runner, Sherpa, Quiver) get their own Performance Max with ROAS targets, accessories and the Bondi beach chair run in Standard Shopping, and brand search is protected separately.

Why: each product group gets a budget and target that fits its margin.

5

Report every week

A short weekly update covering spend, revenue, ROAS by campaign and product, what changed and what is next.

Why: you always know where every dollar went and what it returned.
